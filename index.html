Hi, I’m Zhou Lanxin, from Liaoyuan, China, currently studying at Beijing Normal University.

I spend a lot of time thinking about logic, math, code and questions that probably did not need to become this complicated. I like pulling things apart, following unnecessary side paths, and occasionally finding something useful at the end of them.

I’m not very good at giving myself a neat definition, and I’m not particularly interested in trying. This site is mostly a place for whatever I’m working on, thinking about, or temporarily obsessed with.

Works / Thesis

Master's Thesis

A Geometric Approach to the Axiomatization of Special Relativity

Beijing Normal University Beijing/In Progress

First-order axiomatization of geometric and causal structures in special relativity, with current work on coordinate representation in four-dimensional affine models and the definability of associated algebraic structures.

Bachelor's Thesis

Defending the Indispensability Argument for Mathematical Realism

Jilin University/2024

Examines the Quine–Putnam indispensability argument for mathematical realism, its philosophical foundations, its response to Field’s fictionalism, and a possible revision of the argument.

Minor Degree Thesis

Die Widersprüche der Wagnerschen Ästhetik in „Kunst und Revolution“

Jilin University/2023

Untersucht die inneren Widersprüche in Wagners Ästhetik und Musiktheorie anhand seiner kompositorischen Techniken sowie ihrer philosophischen Kritik bei Nietzsche und Adorno.
